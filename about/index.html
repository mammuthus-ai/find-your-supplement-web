About the project

Evidence-based. Transparent. Free to start.

Find Your Supplement is a personalized supplement recommendation engine built on PubMed research. It started as a mobile app and now has a web version so anyone can access it instantly, without downloading anything.

Why this exists

The problem we're solving

The supplement industry is a $177 billion market filled with marketing claims, proprietary blends, and influencer promotions. Most people don't know which supplements they actually need — and end up spending money on things that do nothing for their specific situation.

The science, however, is clear. Certain nutrients are systematically deficient in certain diets. Lifestyle factors like low sun exposure, intense exercise, or chronic stress create specific nutritional demands. Symptoms can point to likely deficiencies. None of this is secret — it's all published in peer-reviewed literature.

We built this tool to translate that research into simple, personalized recommendations.

Methodology

How the engine works

Our algorithm is a weighted, multi-dimensional scoring system calibrated against PubMed systematic reviews and clinical diagnostic accuracy data.

01

You answer a short quiz

Five steps covering your symptoms, diet type, lifestyle factors (sun exposure, exercise, alcohol, caffeine, stress), age and sex, and health goals. No account required. Takes about 2 minutes.

02

We score each supplement

Our engine evaluates 26 supplements across five profile dimensions: your diet (the strongest signal), lifestyle factors, reported symptoms, age and sex, and stated goals. Diet, lifestyle and symptoms carry the most weight because they are the best predictors of a real shortfall; goals count for less because they are a preference, not a clinical signal.

03

Results are ranked by relevance

Each supplement gets a match score (0–100) that combines how well it fits your profile with the strength of the research behind it, its clinical-trial record, its safety record and how widely it is available. Great match (80+) means strong support on most of those; Good match (60–79) means solid relevance; Fair match (below 60) is worth knowing about but not a priority.

04

Evidence tiers contextualize quality

Each supplement is tagged with an evidence tier reflecting underlying research quality: Strong = meta-analyses and RCTs, Moderate = multiple controlled trials, Limited = small trials, Weak = preliminary or mechanistic evidence only.

Evidence tiers

What the grades mean

AStrong evidence

Meta-analyses & systematic RCTs

The gold standard. Multiple high-quality randomized controlled trials, often synthesized in systematic reviews and meta-analyses.

BModerate evidence

Multiple controlled trials

Several well-designed RCTs with consistent findings, or a smaller number of large-scale trials.

CLimited evidence

Small or emerging trials

Some clinical evidence exists but is limited in scale, duration, or consistency. More research needed.

DWeak evidence

Preliminary research

Mostly in-vitro, animal studies, or very small human trials. Mechanistically plausible but not yet well-established clinically.

Privacy

Your data stays yours.

Your health data is personal. We treat it that way.

No account needed

The quiz needs no account. If you choose to save your plan, we store your answers in your account. Otherwise they are linked to a random visitor ID, not your name, unless you choose to give us your email.

Kept securely, never sold

We keep your quiz answers and results encrypted on our servers so we can improve our recommendations. We never sell your health data or use it for advertising.

No data sold, ever

We have no ad network and no data broker relationships. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage, and we never sell your data. Revenue comes from Amazon affiliate commissions and a one-time $3.99 unlock in the app.

Transparency

How we make money

The website quiz is free and there is no subscription. Revenue comes from two sources. The first is the Amazon Associates affiliate program: when you click “Buy on Amazon” and make a purchase, we earn a small commission (typically 1–4%) at no extra cost to you. The second is a one-time $3.99 purchase in the mobile app that unlocks the full plan and the app-only features.

Our rankings are driven by your quiz responses, the scientific literature and each supplement's safety record — never by commission rates or brand partnerships. Most links go to the supplement category on Amazon (e.g., “Vitamin D3 supplement”) so you can choose the product that best fits your needs and budget. Where we show specific products, they are picked by our own scoring of dose, form and manufacturing, and we earn the same commission whichever one you choose.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How does Find Your Supplement work?

You take a free 2-minute quiz about your symptoms, diet, lifestyle, age and sex, and health goals. Our scoring engine evaluates 26 supplements across those five dimensions, weighting diet, lifestyle and symptoms most because they best predict a real shortfall. Each supplement gets a match score (0–100) that combines profile fit with the strength of the PubMed research behind it, its clinical-trial record, its safety record and availability, and is tagged with an evidence tier (Strong, Moderate, Limited, or Weak).

Is Find Your Supplement free?

The quiz and your top 3 recommendations are free on the website, with no account and no subscription. The full plan, blood work and genetic insights, the medication interaction checker and brand match scores are in the mobile app for a one-time $3.99 purchase. We also earn a small commission through the Amazon Associates affiliate program when you purchase supplements through our links, at no extra cost to you.

Is my health data private?

Your quiz answers, results and buy-button taps are stored encrypted on our servers, linked to your account in the app or, on the website, to a random visitor ID (or to your account, if you choose to save your plan). Only you and authorized Insquire staff can see them. We never sell your health data or use it for advertising, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Our privacy policy has the details.

What do the evidence tiers (Strong, Moderate, Limited, Weak) mean?

Strong evidence means the supplement is backed by meta-analyses and systematic RCTs (the gold standard). Moderate evidence means multiple controlled trials with consistent findings. Limited evidence means small trials where more research is needed. Weak evidence means preliminary research from in-vitro, animal studies, or very small human trials.

Should I replace my doctor with this quiz?

No. This tool is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not medical advice and should not replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Always speak to your doctor before starting any supplement regimen, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, or taking medications.
