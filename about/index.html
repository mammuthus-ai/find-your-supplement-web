About the project

Evidence-based. Transparent. Free to start.

Find Your Supplement is a personalized supplement recommendation engine built on PubMed research. It started as a mobile app and now has a web version so anyone can access it instantly, without downloading anything.

Why this exists

The problem we're solving

The supplement industry is a $177 billion market filled with marketing claims, proprietary blends, and influencer promotions. Most people don't know which supplements they actually need — and end up spending money on things that do nothing for their specific situation.

The science, however, is clear. Certain nutrients are systematically deficient in certain diets. Lifestyle factors like low sun exposure, intense exercise, or chronic stress create specific nutritional demands. Symptoms can point to likely deficiencies. None of this is secret — it's all published in peer-reviewed literature.

We built this tool to translate that research into simple, personalized recommendations.

Methodology

How the engine works

Our algorithm is a weighted, multi-dimensional scoring system calibrated against PubMed systematic reviews and clinical diagnostic accuracy data.

01

You answer a short quiz

Four steps: what you want help with, your diet, your lifestyle (sun exposure, exercise, alcohol, stress), and your age and sex. No account required. Takes about 2 minutes.

02

Every answer earns points

Each answer you give earns a supplement points: your diet counts most, then what you want help with, then your lifestyle, age and sex. Those points are multiplied by how strong the evidence is for that supplement and that answer (Strong, Moderate or Limited), and an answer the studies do not support earns nothing. A supplement’s score is the total over your answers, so one that helps with several of them climbs.

03

Results are ranked, then shown as a match

Supplements are ranked by their points. The match score (0–100) says how close each one comes to the best match we found for your answers: your top pick sets the scale, and the rest are shown against it, so two supplements with the same points always show the same score. Great match (67 and above) is close to that best match; Good match (33–66) is a solid fit; Fair match (below 33) is worth knowing about but not a priority. The evidence label beside the score says how sure the science is.

04

Evidence labels say how sure we are

Each pick carries one evidence label, Strong, Moderate or Limited: the grade of the answer that earned it the most points, graded from what the studies found for that use, not from how many studies exist. Nothing below Limited is credited.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How does Find Your Supplement work?

You take a free 2-minute quiz in four steps: what you want help with, your diet, your lifestyle, and your age and sex. Each answer earns a supplement points, multiplied by how strong the evidence is for that supplement and that answer (Strong, Moderate or Limited; nothing when the studies do not support it). Supplements are ranked by their total, each one gets a match score (0–100) compared with the best match found for your answers, and each carries one evidence label: Strong, Moderate or Limited.

Is Find Your Supplement free?

The quiz and your top 3 recommendations are free on the website, with no account needed. The full plan, blood work and genetic insights, the medication interaction checker and brand match scores are in the mobile app with a Pro subscription: $3.99 a month, $14.99 for 6 months or $24.99 a year (US prices), renewing automatically until you cancel in the App Store or Google Play. If you bought the earlier one-time unlock, you keep everything it opened. We also earn a commission if you buy through our links, at no extra cost to you. Payments never change our rankings. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Is my health data private?

Your quiz answers are stored encrypted on our servers, linked to your account in the app (on the website, only if you choose to save your plan). Taps on buy buttons are counted with Google Analytics and Firebase, not linked to your account. Only you and authorized Insquire staff can see your saved answers. We never sell your health data or use it for advertising, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Our privacy policy has the details.

What do the evidence labels (Strong, Moderate, Limited) mean?

Each label is graded from what the studies found for that use, not from how many studies exist. Strong means consistent results in good-quality trials or reviews. Moderate means some good trials found a benefit, and more would make it firmer. Limited means small or mixed studies: it may help, but the case is not settled. Nothing below Limited is credited.

Should I replace my doctor with this quiz?

No. This tool is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not medical advice and should not replace consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Always speak to your doctor before starting any supplement regimen, especially if you are pregnant, nursing, or taking medications.
