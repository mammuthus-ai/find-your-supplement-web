Privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Overview

Find Your Supplement is operated by Insquire, LLC ("we", "us", "our"), which is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website (findyoursupplement.com) and mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information (App and Website)

The mobile app requires an account. On the website an account is optional: you only need one if you choose to save your plan (see "Saving Your Plan from the Website" below). It is the same account in both places. In the app you can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password; on the website, with Google or with an email address and password. Depending on the method you choose, we receive your email address, a unique identifier from Apple or Google, and, if you allow it, your name. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" option, we receive only Apple's private relay address. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth on our behalf; passwords are stored only as salted hashes and are never visible to us.

Quiz Answers, Results and Health Data (Mobile App)

When you complete the quiz in the app, your answers (age, sex, health goals, diet type, lifestyle factors, medications, and symptoms) and any blood work values or genetic variant data you enter are saved to your account on our servers, so that your plan follows you between devices and reinstalls. If you answer the optional pregnancy question on your Profile (pregnant, breastfeeding, or trying to get pregnant), that answer is saved to your account in the same way and is used to leave out or flag supplements that are not advised for you. Your current quiz answers are saved to your account and replaced when you re-run the quiz; your recommendations are worked out from them each time you open the app and are not stored. A copy of your current plan is also kept on your device so the app works offline.

This data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Database access rules ensure that, apart from you, only authorized Insquire, LLC personnel can read it. We review saved quiz profiles, individually and in aggregate, to run and support the service, to check that recommendations are accurate, and to improve our recommendations and the app. You can delete everything at any time (see "Deleting Your Account" below).

Quiz Answers and Results (Website)

No account is needed to use the website quiz. When you complete it, your answers (the same kinds of information as in the app, including health goals, symptoms, and age and sex) are sent to our recommendation service only to work out your results; they are not stored there and are not linked to any identifier. They are kept in your browser so your results page works, and in your account only if you choose to save your plan (see below). We keep anonymous running totals of quiz completions, quiz-link clicks and buy-button taps. If you give us your email address on the website (for example to subscribe to our newsletter), it goes to ConvertKit (see "Email" below) and is not linked to your answers.

Saving Your Plan from the Website

On the results page you can choose to save your plan to an account, so the app opens with it. We do this only after you tick the box that says "Save my quiz answers, including health information, to my account." We then store your quiz answers (symptoms, diet, lifestyle, age, sex, and health goals) in your account, the same way the app stores them. If the account already has a plan, we ask before replacing it, and we keep the medications, lab values, and genetic data you entered in the app. We also keep a record of your consent: the date, the version of this policy, and that you gave it on the website.

Your browser keeps a sign-in token in its local storage until you sign out, so you stay signed in on the website. While you finish signing in (for example after confirming your email), it also keeps a copy of your answers, which is removed once your plan is saved or you sign out. If you don't finish, the copy is no longer used after 24 hours and is deleted the next time you open our website. You can delete the saved plan, the consent record, and the account at any time (see "Deleting Your Account" below).

Buy Buttons (Website and App)

When you tap a "Buy" or "Shop on Amazon" button, our analytics records the tap (see "Analytics" below): which supplement the button was for and its rank in your results and, in the app, where you tapped it and, in a list of products, the product's place in that list. Apart from an anonymous running total of buy-button taps on the website, we do not keep our own record of your taps on our servers, and these analytics records are not linked to your account, your name, or your email address. We use them, in aggregate, to understand which recommendations lead people to act. Amazon does not tell us who completes a purchase, so we do not know whether you bought anything after tapping a link; Amazon reports only total orders to us.

AI-Assisted Features

If you upload a lab report PDF in the mobile app, the PDF is sent through our server to OpenAI's API so the supported lab values can be read from it. The whole PDF is sent, including anything else printed on it, such as your name and date of birth. We do not store the PDF, and we ask OpenAI not to store the request. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models (per their API data usage policy), but it may keep API requests for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, or longer where the law requires it or where OpenAI needs it to protect its services or others from harm. Only the values you choose to save are kept in your account.

If you upload a raw DNA file, the app reads the file on your device and sends only the lines for the specific genetic markers we analyze to our server, which works out your variants and then discards those lines. Your DNA data is not sent to OpenAI, and your full genome file never leaves your device.

If you scan a supplement label in the mobile app, the photos you take or choose (up to four per scan) are sent through our server to OpenAI's API so the label's ingredients and amounts can be read. Each photo is sent as it is, including anything else in the picture. We do not store the photos, and we ask OpenAI not to store the request. OpenAI does not use API data to train its models (per their API data usage policy), but it may keep API requests for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, or longer where the law requires it or where OpenAI needs it to protect its services or others from harm. What was read from the label, with any corrections you make, is saved only on the device where you scanned it, for your account, until you remove the scan, clear the app's data, or delete your account on that device; we keep your 20 most recent scans and drop older ones automatically. To prevent abuse, we keep a daily count of how many labels your account submits for reading; the count contains no photos or label content, is kept for up to 30 days, and is deleted with your account.

Each account can have up to three lab reports and three DNA files read automatically. To enforce this, we keep a count of the automatic readings your account has used for lab reports and for DNA files, with the dates on which each count was first and last increased (a reading we give back because automatic reading was down or busy lowers the count but does not change these dates). These counts and dates contain no lab values, genetic results or file content, and are kept until you delete your account.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage access to Pro features; RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt and, once you are signed in, your account identifier so that a purchase is restored on any device where you sign in. RevenueCat holds your Pro status against that account identifier, and the app reads it from RevenueCat; we do not keep our own purchase records. We never see your payment card details.

Analytics

Separately from the records above, which we keep on our own servers, we use Google Analytics 4 (website) and Firebase Analytics (mobile app) to collect usage data such as screen views, quiz completion rates, and button taps. Events may include high-level quiz categories (the goals and diet type you selected) and which supplements you viewed or tapped a buy button for, but never your lab values, genetic data, symptoms, medications, pregnancy answer, or account details are sent to Google. These events are not linked to your account: we do not send Google your account identifier, name, or email address. On the website, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (for example _ga) in your browser to tell visits apart. You can turn off analytics in the app's Settings, or on the website by using a browser extension like Google Analytics Opt-out.

Email

When you create an account in the mobile app, the sign-in screen shows an "Email me supplement tips and updates" box. Where the law allows it (for example the United States) the box is ticked by default and we add your email address (and your first name, if your sign-in provider shares it) to our newsletter (supplement tips and product updates, sent via ConvertKit) unless you untick it. In regions that require opt-in consent (the EU and EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Canada and Australia) the box starts unticked and we add you only if you tick it. If you voluntarily provide your email address on the website or in the app's Learn tab (for example by subscribing to new articles), we store it in ConvertKit the same way. The choice you make with that box is stored on your account so that we act on it once and can honour it across your devices. Creating an account or signing in on the website does not add you to the newsletter. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Account emails (confirmation and password reset, from the app or the website) are separate: they are sent by our authentication provider, are not marketing messages, and are always sent regardless of your newsletter choice.

Your Consent to Health Data Collection

Your answers about symptoms, medications, pregnancy, health goals, blood work and genetics are health data. We collect and use them only with your consent: by completing the quiz on the website, or by agreeing when you create an account or when the app asks you, you consent to the collection, storage, and use of your health data as described in this policy. Saving your plan from the website to an account needs its own tick-box consent, which we record. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or by emailing us (see "Your Rights" below); withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in
  • To generate personalized supplement recommendations based on your quiz answers
  • To keep your plan, lab results, and genetic data in sync across your devices
  • To restore your purchases and unlock Pro features you have paid for
  • To improve our recommendation algorithm and app experience
  • To review saved quiz profiles, individually and in aggregate, and buy-button taps in aggregate, so that we understand which recommendations people receive and act on
  • To send you newsletter content, according to the choice you made with the newsletter box when creating an app account or by opting in on the website
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns (via Google Analytics and Firebase)

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not share your health data with advertisers
  • We do not use your health data for advertising or share it for anyone else's marketing
  • We do not require an account to use the website quiz
  • We do not track you across other websites

Service Providers

We rely on a small number of providers to run the service: Supabase (authentication, database, the recommendation service, and processing of lab report, DNA and supplement-label uploads), Apple and Google (sign-in and app stores), RevenueCat (purchase validation), OpenAI (reading lab report PDFs and supplement-label photos you upload), Google Analytics and Firebase (analytics), Expo (delivery of app updates), and ConvertKit (email). Each processes data only as needed to provide its service to us.

Affiliate Links

Our website and app contain Amazon affiliate links (Amazon Associates store mammuthus-20). When you click these links and make a purchase, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon may collect data about your purchase according to their own privacy policy.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from the app: open Settings, choose Delete Account, and confirm. This permanently removes your account, your quiz profile, any lab or genetic data stored with it, and your consent records, and revokes the Sign in with Apple or Google authorization. Deletion cannot be undone. An account made on the website is the same kind of account and is deleted the same way: sign in to the app with it, or email us. If you cannot access the app, email us at the address below from the email on your account and we will delete it for you. Purchase records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat are retained as required for accounting and refunds. Step-by-step instructions are on our account deletion page.

Data Retention

Account data, quiz profile, and consent records: retained until you delete your account. Website quiz answers: not stored on our servers; they stay in your browser for the session. Email addresses (newsletter): retained until you unsubscribe. Analytics data: retained by Google for up to 14 months. Lab-report and DNA-file reading counts, with the dates each count was first and last increased: retained until you delete your account. Lab report PDFs: not stored by us; OpenAI may keep API requests for up to 30 days, or longer in the cases described under AI-Assisted Features. DNA marker lines: not stored. Supplement-label photos: not stored by us; OpenAI may keep API requests for up to 30 days, or longer in the cases described under AI-Assisted Features. Scanned label details: only on the device where you scanned them, until you remove the scan, clear the app's data, or delete your account on that device (your 20 most recent scans are kept). Label-reading usage counts: up to 30 days, and deleted with your account.

Your Rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal and health data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent, by emailing us at the address under "Contact Us". We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Depending on where you live (for example Washington, Nevada, Connecticut, California, or the EU and UK) you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honour them.

Consumer Health Data

This section summarizes how we handle consumer health data, as required by laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. What we collect: health goals, symptoms, medications, age and sex, lifestyle factors, pregnancy or breastfeeding status (if you choose to answer), blood work values and genetic variants you enter, and which supplements you tap a buy button for (recorded in our analytics, not linked to your account; see "Analytics" above). The supplements recommended to you are worked out from these answers and are not stored. Where it comes from: directly from you, through the quiz and the app. Why: to give you recommendations and keep your plan, to run and improve the service, and to understand which recommendations people act on, as described above.Who receives it: only the service providers listed above (chiefly Supabase, which hosts our database), who process it on our behalf; we do not sell it. Your rights: see "Your Rights" above.

Children's Privacy

Find Your Supplement is built for adults and is not meant for anyone under 18; our Terms of Service require every user to be at least 18. A parent or guardian who believes that someone under 18 has given us personal information, for example by creating an account, should contact us at the email address under "Contact Us" below. We will delete that information where the law requires us to.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. If we change how we use health data we already hold in a material way, we will ask for your consent before applying the change to that data.
