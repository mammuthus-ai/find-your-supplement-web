Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Overview

Find Your Supplement is operated by Insquire, LLC ("we", "us", "our"), which is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website (findyoursupplement.com) and mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information (Mobile App)

The mobile app requires an account. You can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password. Depending on the method you choose, we receive your email address, a unique identifier from Apple or Google, and, if you allow it, your name. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" option, we receive only Apple's private relay address. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth on our behalf; passwords are stored only as salted hashes and are never visible to us.

Quiz Answers, Results and Health Data (Mobile App)

When you complete the quiz in the app, your answers (age, sex, health goals, diet type, lifestyle factors, medications, and symptoms) and any blood work values or genetic variant data you enter are saved to your account on our servers, so that your plan follows you between devices and reinstalls. We also keep a record of every quiz you complete, linked to your account: the answers you gave, the date, and the recommendations you received (which supplements were suggested, their ranking and match scores). A copy of your current plan is also kept on your device so the app works offline.

This data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Database access rules ensure that, apart from you, only authorized Insquire, LLC personnel can read it. We review these records, including individual records, to run and support the service, to check that recommendations are accurate, and to understand which recommendations people receive and which products they choose, so that we can improve our recommendations and the app. You can delete everything at any time (see "Deleting Your Account" below).

Quiz Answers and Results (Website)

No account is needed to use the website quiz. When you complete it, your answers (the same kinds of information as in the app, including health goals, symptoms, and age and sex) and the recommendations you received are sent to our servers and stored there. Because you are not signed in, we link these records to a random visitor identifier that is saved in your browser's local storage, not to your name. If you give us your email address on the website (for example to subscribe to our newsletter), we link that email address to the records made with the same visitor identifier, before and after you give it. We use these records for the same purposes as the app records described above. A copy of your answers is also kept in your browser so your results page works.

Buy Buttons and Activity Records (Website and App)

When you tap a "Buy" or "Shop on Amazon" button, we record which supplement and product you chose, where in the app or website you tapped it, the platform (website, iPhone, or Android), and the time. In the app these records are linked to your account; on the website they are linked to your visitor identifier (and to your email address if you have given it to us). We use them to understand which recommendations lead people to act. Amazon does not tell us who completes a purchase, so we do not know whether you bought anything after tapping a link; Amazon reports only total orders to us.

AI-Assisted Features

The mobile app does not currently read lab report PDFs or DNA files automatically: lab values and genetic variants are entered by hand and stored with your quiz profile as described above. If we add AI-assisted parsing in the future, we will update this policy before it goes live.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage access to Pro features; RevenueCat receives the store's purchase receipt and, once you are signed in, your account identifier so that a purchase is restored on any device where you sign in. RevenueCat also notifies us of each purchase, and we keep a record of it linked to your account (the product, price, store, and date) together with your other records. We never see your payment card details.

Analytics

Separately from the records above, which we keep on our own servers, we use Google Analytics 4 (website) and Firebase Analytics (mobile app) to collect usage data such as screen views, quiz completion rates, and button taps. Events may include high-level quiz categories (the goals and diet type you selected) and which supplements you viewed, but never your lab values, genetic data, symptoms, medications, or account details are sent to Google. On the website, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (for example _ga) in your browser to tell visits apart. You can turn off analytics in the app's Settings, or on the website by using a browser extension like Google Analytics Opt-out.

Email

When you create an account in the mobile app, the sign-in screen shows an "Email me supplement tips and updates" box. Where the law allows it (for example the United States) the box is ticked by default and we add your email address (and your first name, if your sign-in provider shares it) to our newsletter (supplement tips and product updates, sent via ConvertKit) unless you untick it. In regions that require opt-in consent (the EU and EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Canada and Australia) the box starts unticked and we add you only if you tick it. If you voluntarily provide your email address on the website or in the app's Learn tab (for example by subscribing to new articles), we store it in ConvertKit the same way. The choice you make with that box is stored on your account so that we act on it once and can honour it across your devices. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and you can unsubscribe at any time. Account emails from the app (confirmation and password reset) are separate: they are sent by our authentication provider, are not marketing messages, and are always sent regardless of your newsletter choice.

Your Consent to Health Data Collection

Your answers about symptoms, medications, health goals, blood work and genetics are health data. We collect and use them only with your consent: by completing the quiz on the website, or by agreeing when you create an account or when the app asks you, you consent to the collection, storage, and use of your health data as described in this policy. If you already had an account before October 2, 2026, the app will ask for your agreement before we use your existing data in the ways this version describes. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting your account or by emailing us (see "Your Rights" below); withdrawing does not affect processing that took place before.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in
  • To generate personalized supplement recommendations based on your quiz answers
  • To keep your plan, lab results, and genetic data in sync across your devices
  • To restore your purchases and unlock Pro features you have paid for
  • To improve our recommendation algorithm and app experience
  • To review quiz records, recommendations, buy-button activity, and purchases, individually and in aggregate, so that we understand which recommendations people receive and act on
  • To send you newsletter content, according to the choice you made with the newsletter box when creating an app account or by opting in on the website
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns (via Google Analytics and Firebase)

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not share your health data with advertisers
  • We do not use your health data for advertising or share it for anyone else's marketing
  • We do not require an account to use the website quiz
  • We do not track you across other websites

Service Providers

We rely on a small number of providers to run the service: Supabase (authentication, database, and the recommendation service), Apple and Google (sign-in and app stores), RevenueCat (purchase validation), Google Analytics and Firebase (analytics), and ConvertKit (email). Each processes data only as needed to provide its service to us.

Affiliate Links

Our website and app contain Amazon affiliate links (Amazon Associates store mammuthus-20). When you click these links and make a purchase, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon may collect data about your purchase according to their own privacy policy.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from the app: open Settings, choose Delete Account, and confirm. This permanently removes your account, your quiz profile, any lab or genetic data stored with it, and the quiz, buy-button and purchase records linked to your account, and revokes the Sign in with Apple or Google authorization. Deletion cannot be undone. If you cannot access the app, email us at the address below from the email on your account and we will delete it for you. Purchase records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat are retained as required for accounting and refunds. Step-by-step instructions are on our account deletion page.

Data Retention

Account data, quiz profile, and the quiz, buy-button and purchase records linked to your account: retained until you delete your account. Website quiz and buy-button records: retained until you ask us to delete them (if they are linked to your email address) or until we delete them as no longer needed. Email addresses (newsletter): retained until you unsubscribe. Analytics data: retained by Google for up to 14 months.

Your Rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal and health data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and withdraw your consent, by emailing us at the address under "Contact Us". For website records, include the email address you gave us. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Depending on where you live (for example Washington, Nevada, Connecticut, California, or the EU and UK) you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honour them.

Consumer Health Data

This section summarizes how we handle consumer health data, as required by laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. What we collect: health goals, symptoms, medications, age and sex, lifestyle factors, blood work values and genetic variants you enter, the supplements recommended to you, and your buy-button activity. Where it comes from: directly from you, through the quiz and the app. Why: to give you recommendations and keep your plan, to run and improve the service, and to understand which recommendations people act on, as described above.Who receives it: only the service providers listed above (chiefly Supabase, which hosts our database), who process it on our behalf; we do not sell it. Your rights: see "Your Rights" above.

Children's Privacy

Find Your Supplement is built for adults and is not meant for anyone under 18; our Terms of Service require every user to be at least 18. A parent or guardian who believes that someone under 18 has given us personal information, for example by creating an account, should contact us at the email address under "Contact Us" below. We will delete that information where the law requires us to.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised. If we change how we use health data we already hold in a material way, we will ask for your consent before applying the change to that data.
