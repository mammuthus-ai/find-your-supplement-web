Consumer health data privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Find Your Supplement is run by Insquire, LLC. This page covers the health data we collect, as required by Washington's My Health My Data Act. Our full Privacy Policy covers everything else.

Health data we collect, and how we use it

  • Quiz answers: goals, symptoms, diet and lifestyle, age and sex, medicines, and an optional pregnancy or breastfeeding answer. Used to make your recommendations and keep your plan on your devices.
  • Lab values and DNA results you add. Used to tailor your recommendations. Lab reports and DNA files are read once to pull out the values; we don't keep the files.
  • Personal weekly picks (if you sign up): your answers and matches. Used to write your emails.
  • Your consent choices. Used to keep a record of what you agreed to.
  • Only if you tick "Use my answers and my cabinet to help improve the app": your answers and cabinet, counted in totals of at least 10 people and looked at by our staff to improve the app.
  • Only if you turn on "Share usage data": the screens you open and buttons you tap, which show that you use our app. Used to find problems.

Where it comes from

From you (the quiz, your Profile and Settings) and the files and photos you upload.

Who we share it with

We don't sell health data, and we have no affiliates. Companies that process it for us under contract: Supabase (stores it), OpenAI (reads lab reports and label photos), Resend (sends emails you asked for), Google Firebase (app usage data, only if you turn it on) and Google Analytics (visits to our website, including which pages you read), and our internal workspace tools (only for people who ticked "Use my answers and my cabinet to help improve the app").

Your rights

You can see the health data we hold about you, including a list of every third party we've shared it with and how to contact them; delete it (we also tell the companies above to delete it, and backups clear within 8 days); and withdraw any consent. Use the app's Settings or email support@findyoursupplement.com from your account's address. We answer within 45 days, or tell you within that time why we need 45 more.

To appeal a refusal, reply with "Appeal" in the subject; we answer in writing within 45 days. If we still say no, you can complain to the Washington Attorney General.
