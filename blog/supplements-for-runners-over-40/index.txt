That study didn't test middle-aged runners, but aiming for 30–40 g of protein after hard sessions is a reasonable target. Aim for about 1.4–2.0 g/kg/day in total (roughly 100–140 g for a 70 kg runner), per the <a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28642676/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="font-semibold text-ink underline decoration-ember underline-offset-2">ISSN position stand</a>. A <a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34883514/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="font-semibold text-ink underline decoration-ember underline-offset-2">2022 large cohort study</a> found that higher magnesium intake was linked to a lower chance of short sleep (under seven hours), though this kind of study can't prove magnesium caused it. If you try fish oil, 1,000–2,000 mg EPA+DHA daily is a common dose — check the EPA+DHA content, not the total fish oil weight. Doses above 1 g/day slightly raise the risk of atrial fibrillation (<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34612056/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="font-semibold text-ink underline decoration-ember underline-offset-2">Gencer et al., 2021</a>), so check with your doctor if you have heart rhythm problems or take blood thinners such as warfarin.8:T63c,<strong>Creatine monohydrate (evidence: strong for strength training; limited for running).</strong> The <a href="https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12970-017-0173-z" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="font-semibold text-ink underline decoration-ember underline-offset-2">ISSN position stand</a> calls creatine the most researched and effective ergogenic supplement available, with a robust safety record in healthy people. In older adults who lifted weights 2–3 times a week, creatine plus resistance training produced <strong>1.37 kg more lean tissue</strong> than training alone (<a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29138605/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="font-semibold text-ink underline decoration-ember underline-offset-2">Chilibeck et al., 2017</a>).
